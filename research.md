<h5 class="page-opening"> Research Interests</h5>
<p>My research interests lie in quantitative marketing, with a focus on digital platforms, consumer decision-making, and the economics of artificial intelligence. I am interested in how information and incentives shape interactions among consumers, firms, and AI agents.</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>AI and market design:</strong> How AI agents change consumer search, recommendations, negotiation, and competition, and how market rules can align their incentives with those of consumers and firms.</li>
  <li><strong>Digital platforms and content markets:</strong> How content licensing, information sharing, and subscription models affect competition, investment in content quality, and customer relationships.</li>
  <li><strong>Consumer decisions in digital commerce:</strong> How anticipated regret, information, and promotional policies shape engagement and purchase decisions, especially in livestream commerce and influencer marketing.</li>
</ul>
<p>I am especially interested in analytical and game-theoretic modeling, alongside statistical and machine-learning methods for measuring consumer preferences and evaluating AI-assisted market research.</p>
